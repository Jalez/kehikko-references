Kind: fix

- **The relation column is on every row.** A merge request that closes an issue now says "Closes #34" where a partial one says "Part of #12", and the issue says "Closed by !56". With several, the first shows and "+2" counts the rest; hover or a screen reader gets all of them.
- **Rows no longer go uneven.** Every cell of a row (activity, labels, date, relation) is drawn on every row, empty when there is nothing to say, so a missing value can no longer shift the columns after it.
