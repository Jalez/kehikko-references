- **One reference at a time in a tiny container.** When the container is only a couple of rows tall, References shows one reference as a whole card: its state, full title, place in its stack, CI, review and last activity. Scrolling, swiping or the arrow keys move to the next card whole, and the footer says where you are, such as "3 / 12".
- **What you have not seen comes first.** In that view, references with new activity you have not looked at yet come first, newest first, marked "Unseen". What you have seen is remembered for each kehikko.
- **Right-click to copy.** Right-click a reference, press Shift+F10, or hold it on a touch screen to copy it, everything the list shows, or everything related to it, such as its whole stack, with links or as ids. The footer says how many were copied.
