- **Search the references with ⌘F.** Press ⌘F in References to open Kehikot's find bar: every match in the list is highlighted and counted ("3 of 17"), the list scrolls to the current one, Enter and ⇧Enter move between matches, and Escape closes the bar.
- **Your place in the list is kept.** Each References container remembers how far down the list you had scrolled, and comes back there when you return to the kehikko or reopen the app.
- **No "Reading the trackers" flash.** Kehikot keeps its cover up until the list is drawn; the waiting page shows only when a first read takes a while.
- **A page that fails says so.** If the list cannot be drawn, Kehikot is told what went wrong instead of showing a blank container.
