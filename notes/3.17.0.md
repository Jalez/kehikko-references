- **Forked stacks show as a tree.** When two changes are built on the same one, the stack marker shows a fork sign and how many changes it holds, and its list draws the branches with tree lines instead of one long numbered line. A stack without a fork still reads "2/3".
- **Copies bring the stack.** Copying one or more references, with links or as ids, also copies the stacks they are in, bottom first, with forks drawn as a tree and the copied ones marked. Afterwards the selection says what was copied, such as "Copied 2 + stack".
- **Titles stay in narrow containers.** A row now keeps its title at any width, cut short with the full text on hover. To make room, narrow rows have less padding and overlap people's pictures, showing fewer of them with a "+N".
