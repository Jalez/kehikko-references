- **A source that could not be read can be tried again or closed.** The notice has a "Try again" that reads the trackers again ("Reading…" while it runs) and an × that hides it until the source fails differently, or fails again after a good read. The footer has the same two.
- **Plain words for why.** An interrupted or timed-out read, a missing sign-in (with the `glab auth login` / `gh auth login` command to run) and an unreachable host are said plainly; the tool's own text is kept in the notice's tooltip.
