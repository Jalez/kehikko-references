- **Copying no longer adds the stack.** Copy, Copy ids and the copies in the right-click menu now give just the references (and their titles), with nothing after them. Copy ids is only the ids.
- **"With stack" when you want it.** When a selected reference is in a stack, a "With stack" checkbox sits right beside the copy actions: in the selection menu, in the footer next to Copy, and in the right-click menu. It is off until you turn it on, and each kehikko remembers your choice.
- **Copy related is unchanged.** It is about the stack, so it still copies the whole stack.
