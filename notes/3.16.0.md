- **The count and selection sit in the app's own footer.** In a Kehikot app that draws module footers, the line under the list (how many references, of what, how old the reading is, what is unread) and the actions for the selected references (open, copy, copy ids, the epics they are in, clear) appear in the container's footer, in the same look as its header. In an older app the module keeps drawing its own bar as before.
- **Narrow containers keep what matters.** When the container is narrow the footer keeps the count and the clear button, and the rest moves into its "⋯" menu.
- **A copy that cannot reach the clipboard says so.** If a copy pressed in the footer is refused by the clipboard, you are told to press ⌘C in the list instead, which always copies the selected references.
