- **The count and selection sit in the app's own footer.** In a Kehikot app that draws module footers and does copying and opening itself, the line under the list (how many references, of what, how old the reading is, what is unread) and the actions for the selected references (open, copy, copy ids, the epics they are in, clear) appear in the container's footer, in the same look as its header. In an older app the module keeps drawing its own bar and selection menu as before.
- **Copy and Open work from the footer.** Kehikot itself copies the selected references (with links and titles, or just their ids) or opens the selected one in your browser when you press it, so the press always lands. Open in the footer opens one reference at a time.
- **Narrow containers keep what matters.** When the container is narrow the footer keeps the count and the clear button, and the rest moves into its "⋯" menu.
