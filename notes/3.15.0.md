- **See which epics a reference is in.** The selection menu now has an "In epics" section listing every epic of the project that the selected merge requests, pull requests or issues belong to: the epic names them, or an issue they close or are part of. Each row shows the epic's title and its part, and with several selected, how many are in it ("2 of 3"). With none it says "Not in any epic of this project."
- **Jump to the epic.** Pressing an epic in that section takes you there; the epic you are in is marked "open now". The epics are read when you open the menu.
