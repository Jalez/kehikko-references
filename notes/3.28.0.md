- **Agents hear about new activity on the epic's references.** When someone else reviews, requests changes, comments on a line, pushes commits, merges, closes or reopens one of the epic's references, or its CI fails or recovers, the agents working at that epic on this kehikko are told in a short line, such as "Mona requested changes on !1801 · 2 threads on api.ts". Each event is told once, even across restarts, and never your own.
- **A switch for it.** "Tell agents about new activity" in the View menu turns it off for a kehikko, and the choice is kept. While it is off nothing is told, and turning it back on does not replay what happened meanwhile.
- **For agents:** sessions at an epic are told about new review, comment, commit, CI, merge and close activity on its items, at most one message a minute, the rest gathered into one summary.
