- **Copy several references at once.** With two or more rows selected, "Copy N references" puts one line per reference on the clipboard, in the order the list shows them, each linked with its title. Its small menu copies just the ids instead, ready to hand to an agent. Cmd+C does the same while rows are selected.
- **The open icon opens your own browser.** The icon at the end of a row now opens the issue or merge request in your browser, outside the app. Clicking the row still shows it in the app.
